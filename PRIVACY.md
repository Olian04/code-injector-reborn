
# Privacy Policy of **Code Injector Reborn**

**Oliver Anteros** (Owner) maintains **Code Injector Reborn** (Application), a fork of *Code Injector* by **L. Sabatelli**, as a Free app.  

No data is collected, transmitted or shared. The Application contains no analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

To decide which rules to inject, the Application reads the address of the page you are visiting and compares it against your rules. That address is used in the moment and never stored or sent anywhere. Your rules — including the code you write in the Editor — are stored only in the browser's local extension storage on your device.

The only network requests the Application makes are the ones your own rules ask for: fetching a remote file that a rule references, or importing a rule list from a URL you provide.

## Permissions

- **Read your browsing history** - Required to know which page is being loaded, so the matching rules can be injected. Nothing is recorded.
- **Storage** - Used for accessing and storing your rules and settings on your device.

## Security

Your data is 100% safe as it is saved and processed locally in your device.
